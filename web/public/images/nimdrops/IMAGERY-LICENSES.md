## Corrections

**2026-07-27 — `red-packet-cutout-256.avif` re-encoded.** The first emit of this
one file decoded to a pure-black colour plane behind a correct alpha mask: mean
RGB `(0, 0, 0)` against `(72, 23, 11)` for its own WebP twin, and against a
correct `red-packet-cutout-426.avif`. Every browser prefers the AVIF source and
`(max-width: 480px)` selects the 256 candidate, so the landing hero rendered as
a black silhouette of the envelope on every phone. Re-encoded from
`red-packet-cutout-426.webp`, resampled to 256 x 252 with Lanczos, `avifenc -q 62
-s 4`. The decoded mean now matches the WebP exactly. No other emitted file was
affected; all twenty were re-checked by decoding each one and comparing its mean
RGBA against its WebP counterpart.
